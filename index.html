01 / About

Building things
with purpose.

I'm Electrical Captain at UCD Formula Student, leading a division of 19 engineers responsible for the car's electronics, and still the team's lead programmer: the battery management system, the vehicle control unit, and the CAN network that ties them together. I was the first Computer Science student on the team. The intersection of software and physical systems is where I'm most at home.

Across the 2026 season I moved our VCU onto the Teensy 4.1 platform, wrote the firmware for our custom STM32 battery management system with a protocol-level simulator so it could be tested before hardware arrived, and built the internal platform the team now runs on. We took E135 to FSUK 2026 at Silverstone and finished 29th overall as the top Republic of Ireland team, clearing five scrutineering stickers — four of them a first for UCD.

Alongside that I'm a final-year Computer Science (Data Science & AI) student at UCD, holding a 3.97 / 4.20 GPA, First Class Honours. I spent the summer of 2025 as a Software Engineer Intern at Twilio in Dublin, working on high-throughput APNs messaging infrastructure in Java.
