01 / About

Building things
with purpose.

I'm Powertrain Captain at UCD Formula Student, where I lead the software side of our electric race car: the vehicle control unit, the battery management system, and the CAN network that ties them together. The intersection of software and physical systems is where I'm most at home.

Across the 2026 season I moved our VCU onto the Teensy 4.1 platform, wrote an STM32 battery management stack with a full simulation harness so it could be validated before a single board was flashed, and built the internal tools site the team now runs on. We took E135 to FSUK 2026 at Silverstone and finished 29th overall as the top Republic of Ireland team, clearing five scrutineering stickers — four of them a first for UCD.

Alongside that I'm a final-year Computer Science (Data Science & AI) student at UCD, holding a 4.04 / 4.20 GPA, First Class Honours. I spent the summer of 2025 as a Software Engineer Intern at Twilio in Dublin, working on high-throughput APNs messaging infrastructure in Java.
